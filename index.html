FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why choose Comics Now! over Komga, Kavita, or Ubooquity?

Komga and Kavita are great traditional digital bookshelves, but neither provides AI Panel & Speech Bubble Zoom (Guided View), the GVE Now! visual panel editor, an interactive Visual Crossover Reading Order Map for multi-series events, or a built-in multi-source metadata tagger with Google Gemini Vision cover recognition. Comics Now! also runs without a heavy Java Virtual Machine and requires zero paid subscriptions.

How does CBR and PDF to CBZ conversion work in v3.1.0?

Comics Now! converts RAR (.cbr) and .pdf files into streamable .cbz archives with full page integrity verification. In Settings → Library Preferences, you have two independent controls to either Keep Original Files (the safe default) or Delete Originals After Conversion separately for CBRs and PDFs.

Do I need a local GPU for AI Guided View panel detection?

No! While Comics Now! can run its AI detectors locally on your CPU or GPU, it also integrates natively with Modal Serverless Cloud GPUs to process comic pages in the cloud without slowing down your home server.
